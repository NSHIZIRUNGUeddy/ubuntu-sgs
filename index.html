FAQ

How do I order?

Pick your size, tap Order on WhatsApp, and send the pre-filled message. We confirm availability, price and delivery with you.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver in Kigali and can arrange delivery elsewhere in Rwanda. Ask us on WhatsApp for rates.

How can I pay?

We confirm payment options with you on WhatsApp when you place your order.

Can I exchange the size?

Message us as soon as you receive your order and we will sort out an exchange if stock allows.
